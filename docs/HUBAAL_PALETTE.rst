Hubaal public-site palette
=========================

The owner-supplied palette is defined in frontend/src/palette.js:
Deep teal #075E63, soft mint #D9F1E8, warm ivory #F7F5EF and ink #142D30.

Tailwind brand, teal and ink roles use these values. Public backgrounds, buttons,
navigation, brand marks and diagram accents follow the same palette. Supporting
text and borders use derived tones; warning and error colours retain their
status meaning. Typography roles, page wording and motion are preserved.

Deep teal on warm ivory has contrast 6.90:1. White on deep teal is 7.53:1;
soft mint on ink is 12.22:1. Deep teal action buttons therefore use light text.
Operational backend and payment execution behaviour are unchanged.
